Lemon Squeezy closed new sign-ups on November 16, 2026 and stops processing payments, including subscription renewals, on March 31, 2027. Creem is a Merchant of Record like Lemon Squeezy, so you keep selling worldwide without registering for sales tax or VAT yourself. Your AI agent can do most of the move for you.

| Date              | Lemon Squeezy                                                                                  |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| November 16, 2026 | Stopped accepting new stores                                                                   |
| Mid-January 2027  | Recommended latest date to start migrating. Lemon Squeezy says the move can take up to 8 weeks |
| March 31, 2027    | Stops processing payments, including subscription renewals. No extension is planned            |

Sources: [Saying goodbye to Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/blog/saying-goodbye-to-lemon-squeezy) and the [Lemon Squeezy migration guide](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/migration).

## Before you start

Four checks, about 5 minutes:

1. **What you sell is allowed.** Creem reviews every account before it takes live payments. See [account reviews](/merchant-of-record/account-reviews/account-reviews).
2. **Your country gets payouts.** Creem pays out to merchants in 143 countries and territories. See [supported countries](/merchant-of-record/supported-countries).
3. **Your prices can be in USD or EUR.** If your Lemon Squeezy store uses another currency, decide your new prices first.
4. **Your customers can pay by card.** Creem's checkout takes cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, but not PayPal. Lemon Squeezy can't move PayPal subscriptions to any provider, so those subscribers pay by card on Creem. See [payment methods](/merchant-of-record/finance/payment-methods).

## With your AI agent

This works with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any coding agent that can read web pages and work in your codebase.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Put your API keys in your environment">
    Create an API key in Lemon Squeezy under **Settings → API**, and a Creem test key
    (`creem_test_…`) in [test mode](/getting-started/test-mode). Set them as `LEMONSQUEEZY_API_KEY`
    and `CREEM_API_KEY` in the shell your agent uses. Never paste a key into the chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give your agent the migration instructions">
    Open your agent in your app's repository and paste:

    ```text
    Migrate this app's Lemon Squeezy store to Creem.

    Follow https://docs.creem.io/getting-started/migrate/lemon-squeezy/technical-guide.md
    and the migration prompt in it.

    - My keys are in LEMONSQUEEZY_API_KEY and CREEM_API_KEY. Never print, log or commit them.
    - Use Creem test mode (https://test-api.creem.io) until I approve going live.
    - Start with a read-only inventory of my Lemon Squeezy store and this codebase, then show
      me a plan. Don't create, change or cancel anything until I approve it.
    ```

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the plan, then let it run">
    Your agent shows what it will create, what you finish in the dashboard and what can't move.
    Once you approve, it rebuilds your catalog and customers in test mode, switches new sales to
    Creem in your code, and lists your subscribers by renewal date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Once your account is approved, create a live API key and ask your agent to run the import again
    with it. Your existing subscribers move after that, one at a time, close to their renewal date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **A Lemon Squeezy migration skill is on its way.** Install it once and your agent knows the whole
  migration, with no prompt to paste. Until then, the prompt above gives your agent the same rules.
</Info>

<Tip>
  Connect Creem's [hosted MCP server](/ai/for-agents/hosted-mcp) as well, so your agent can look up
  your Creem store while it works.
</Tip>

## With the Creem CLI

For your catalog alone, the [Creem CLI](/code/cli) copies products and discount codes in one command. It reads your Lemon Squeezy store, shows a plan, and creates the products and codes in the Creem store you're logged in to.

```bash
brew tap armitage-labs/creem
brew install creem
creem login --api-key creem_test_YOUR_KEY
creem migrate lemon-squeezy --dry-run
creem migrate lemon-squeezy
```

| Option                | What it does                             |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| `--ls-api-key <key>`  | Your Lemon Squeezy API key               |
| `--ls-store-id <id>`  | The Lemon Squeezy store to read          |
| `--dry-run`           | Shows the plan without creating anything |
| `--exclude-discounts` | Copies products only                     |

What the CLI copies, and what you finish by hand:

- One product per variant that has a price, with name, price, currency, one-time or recurring, and billing period.
- Published discount codes, applied to all the products it created. Codes limited to some products are skipped.
- It sets every product's tax category to `saas`, copies descriptions as HTML, gives pay-what-you-want variants a fixed price (the minimum or suggested price), and doesn't copy images, trials, files, license key settings or customers.
- It skips variants without a price and billing cadences other than monthly, every 3 or 6 months and yearly.

Fix those fields with [`PATCH /v1/products/{id}`](/api-reference/endpoint/update-product) or in the dashboard. The [technical guide](/getting-started/migrate/lemon-squeezy/technical-guide#3-rebuild-your-catalog) has the field mapping, and covers customers, checkout and webhooks, which the CLI doesn't move.

## What moves

| Moves to Creem                                                                                                                                                  | Doesn't move yet                                                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Products and prices, discount codes, customers, webhooks, checkout links, file downloads and license key settings. Affiliates are set up again in the dashboard | Active subscriptions and saved cards, existing license keys, order history and affiliate referral history |

**Existing subscribers** move one at a time: each one checks out once on Creem, close to their Lemon Squeezy renewal date, so nobody pays twice. A direct import that keeps their renewal date is in development. See [Move existing subscribers](/getting-started/migrate/lemon-squeezy/technical-guide#6-move-existing-subscribers).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do my subscribers need to enter their card again?">
    Yes, for now. Each subscriber checks out once on Creem. Time it close to their Lemon Squeezy
    renewal date so they don't pay twice for the same period. Moving saved cards from Lemon Squeezy
    is in development: if you want it, email support@creem.io before you fill in Lemon Squeezy's
    migration intake form, and we tell you what to put in it. Until we confirm a subscriber's card
    is on Creem, that subscriber keeps renewing on Lemon Squeezy, so don't cancel anything there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can my customers keep their license keys?">
    Not on Creem: Creem issues new keys for purchases on Creem. Keep validating Lemon Squeezy keys
    in your backend until those customers move: ask Creem first, and ask Lemon Squeezy only when
    Creem answers `404`. See [license
    keys](/getting-started/migrate/lemon-squeezy/technical-guide#license-keys).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my order history?">
    It stays in Lemon Squeezy. Export it as CSV before March 31, 2027. Creem shows only the orders
    it processes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does importing customers notify them?">
    No. Creating a customer in Creem sends no email and creates no order.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to finish before March 31, 2027?">
    Move new sales well before then. Every subscriber should have moved by that date: Lemon Squeezy
    stops processing renewals on March 31, 2027 and doesn't plan to extend it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
