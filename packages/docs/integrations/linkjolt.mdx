## Overview

[LinkJolt](https://www.linkjolt.io/integrations/creem-affiliate-tracking?utm_source=creem&utm_medium=docs)
is affiliate tracking and management software with a native Creem connection. An affiliate shares a
link or a discount code, LinkJolt records the click, and when that visitor buys through your Creem
checkout the sale arrives on a signed webhook and is credited to the right affiliate.

Setup is one API key. You paste it into LinkJolt, and LinkJolt adds its own webhook to your Creem
store and keeps the signing secret, so there is no URL to copy and no secret to paste. Sales,
subscription renewals, trials, refunds and chargebacks all arrive through that webhook.

## How It Works

Creem hosts checkout on its own domain, so the affiliate referral needs a way to travel from your
site into the sale. It travels as one metadata field, `linkjolt_ref`, and on Creem payment links the
LinkJolt tracking script adds it for you.

- A visitor clicks an affiliate's link and lands on your site. The LinkJolt tracking script stores
  the referral in their browser for 30 days and exposes it as `window.linkjolt.referral`.
- They go to checkout with the referral attached as `linkjolt_ref` metadata. The script adds it to
  every Creem payment link and product link on your page; if your server creates the checkout, you
  add it there.
- Creem processes the payment and sends a signed webhook to LinkJolt.
- LinkJolt matches the referral, or the discount code used at checkout, to the affiliate,
  calculates the commission, and records it as pending until you approve it.

Your API key sets up and looks after the webhook, and creates discount codes in your store. Sales
never come through the API; they arrive on the signed webhook.

<Note>
  LinkJolt reads `linkjolt_ref` from checkout metadata, not `creem_ref`. Creem reserves `creem_ref`
  for its own [built-in affiliate feature](/features/affiliate-program), so the two can coexist.
</Note>

## Integration Steps

Six steps, about fifteen minutes including a test purchase. You connect Creem's Test Mode first,
check a purchase end to end, then connect your live store.

<Steps>

<Step title="Create a campaign in LinkJolt">

Sign up at [linkjolt.io](https://www.linkjolt.io/auth/sign-up?utm_source=creem&utm_medium=docs) and
create a campaign. It holds your commission terms:

- **Commission**: a percentage of each sale, or a fixed amount.
- **Renewals**: whether subscription renewals pay commission too, and for how many months or
  payments. Recurring commission is on the Professional plan and above.
- **Approval**: whether affiliates who apply are approved automatically, or wait for you.

Then invite affiliates from LinkJolt, or share your program's join page so they can apply.

</Step>

<Step title="Create a restricted API key in Creem">

In your Creem dashboard, switch on **Test Mode**, go to **Developers > API Keys** and create a key.
Switch **Full access** off and grant only the scopes LinkJolt uses:

| Scope                                  | Used for                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `webhooks:read` and `webhooks:write`   | Adding the LinkJolt webhook to your store, reading its signing secret, and switching the webhook back on if it is ever disabled |
| `discounts:read` and `discounts:write` | Creating a discount code for each affiliate                                                                                     |
| `products:read`                        | Listing your active products, so each new code applies to them                                                                  |

Nothing else is required. LinkJolt never changes your products, customers or orders.

A Test Mode key starts with `creem_test_`. Sales from your Test Mode are recorded as test sales,
which are never paid out. You create the live key the same way in step 6.

</Step>

<Step title="Connect Creem in LinkJolt">

In LinkJolt, open **Integrations** and choose the **Creem** tab. Paste the key into **Creem API
key** and click **Set it up**. LinkJolt then:

- adds a webhook named **LinkJolt** to your Creem store, subscribed to every event
- reads the webhook back from Creem to confirm it was saved as asked
- reads the webhook's signing secret, so there is nothing to copy
- keeps the key encrypted, to create discount codes and to check the webhook once a day

The tab now shows your Test Mode as connected. The new webhook is in Creem under **Developers >
Webhooks**; leave its events as they are.

<Tip>
  Rather not hand LinkJolt an API key? Choose **Set it up by hand instead** on the Creem tab, add
  the webhook in Creem yourself with every event selected, and paste its signing secret into
  LinkJolt. Tracking works the same, without automatic discount codes and the daily check.
</Tip>

</Step>

<Step title="Add the LinkJolt tracking script to your site">

The Creem tab also asks how your customers pay on Creem: payment links on your site, a checkout
your own code creates, or discount codes only. For the first two, add the tracking script to the
`<head>` of your marketing site. It records affiliate clicks and stores the referral for the next
step.

```html Tracking script
<script src="https://www.linkjolt.io/api/tracking.js?id=YOUR_MERCHANT_ID"></script>
```

The Creem tab shows the script with your ID filled in, plus a version for React. With discount
codes only, there is nothing to install: skip to step 6.

</Step>

<Step title="Pass the referral into checkout">

This is the step that connects a click to a sale. Pick whichever matches how you sell.

**Payment links**: nothing to add. The tracking script appends `metadata[linkjolt_ref]` to every
link on the page that points at a Creem payment link or product page (`creem.io/payment/`,
`creem.io/product/` and their `/test/` forms), including links added after the page loads. Creem
carries the metadata into the checkout.

**Checkout API or SDK**: read the referral in the browser with `window.linkjolt.referral`, send it
along with your checkout request, and set it as `metadata.linkjolt_ref` where your server creates
the checkout.

<CodeGroup>

```json Checkout API
{
  "product_id": "prod_your_product",
  "success_url": "https://yoursite.com/success",
  "metadata": { "linkjolt_ref": "REFERRAL_FROM_YOUR_FRONTEND" }
}
```

```ts TypeScript SDK
const checkout = await creem.checkouts.create({
  productId: "prod_your_product",
  successUrl: "https://yoursite.com/success",
  metadata: { linkjolt_ref: referral }, // the value of window.linkjolt.referral
});
// send the customer to checkout.checkoutUrl
```

```js Links you build in JavaScript
const url = new URL("https://www.creem.io/payment/prod_your_product");
const referral = window.linkjolt?.referral;
if (referral) url.searchParams.set("metadata[linkjolt_ref]", referral);
window.location.href = url.toString();
```

</CodeGroup>

**Links you build in JavaScript**: the script rewrites links on the page, not a URL your code opens
(for example with `window.open` or `location.href`). Add the parameter yourself, as in the third
tab.

<Tip>
  Creem carries checkout metadata onto the order and the subscription, so renewals keep crediting
  the same affiliate without anything extra.
</Tip>

</Step>

<Step title="Verify with a test purchase, then go live">

Run this once in Test Mode before relying on it. It takes about five minutes.

1. **Visit your site through an affiliate link.** Take any affiliate's link from LinkJolt, or a test
   affiliate's, and open your site with it.
2. **Check the link carries the referral.** Hover a Creem payment link on the page: its address now
   includes `linkjolt_ref`. Checkout API integrations should log the value they send instead.
3. **Buy with a test card.** Complete the checkout with card `4111 1111 1111 1111`, any future
   expiry and any CVC ([test cards](/getting-started/test-mode)). In LinkJolt, the sale appears under
   **Test sales** on the **Commissions** page, credited to that affiliate with its commission.
4. **Refund the purchase in Creem.** LinkJolt reverses the commission.
5. **Resend the webhook.** In Creem, resend the `checkout.completed` delivery. LinkJolt keys every
   payment by its Creem transaction, so nothing changes: still one sale, no second commission.

To go live, switch Test Mode off in Creem, create a live API key with the same scopes as step 2, and
paste it into the same field on LinkJolt's Creem tab. LinkJolt adds its webhook to your live store.
Your Test Mode connection stays, and test sales stay apart from your live commissions.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Affiliate discount codes

Discount codes credit an affiliate without a link, which suits podcasts, videos and newsletters.
Once your live store is connected with an API key:

- **One code at a time**: on the **Coupons** tab of LinkJolt's **Campaigns** page, create a coupon,
  pick the affiliate, choose **Creem discount**, and enter the code and the discount. LinkJolt
  creates it as a discount in your Creem store, with the percentage or amount and the duration you
  chose.
- **A code for every affiliate**: switch on **A coupon code for every affiliate** in a campaign's
  settings, and every approved affiliate gets their own code, named after them.

A sale that uses the code is credited to that affiliate, by the discount's ID or by its code.
LinkJolt creates each code for the products active in your store at that moment, so a product you
add later needs new codes.

Discounts you create in Creem yourself work too: register each one on the same Coupons tab by its
`dis_` ID.

## What Gets Tracked

Once you are connected, these all happen on their own:

| Event                      | What happens                                                                            |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One-time sale              | Commission recorded from `checkout.completed`, pending until you approve it             |
| First subscription payment | Recorded once, whether `checkout.completed` or `subscription.paid` arrives first        |
| Subscription renewal       | Commission from each `subscription.paid`, at your recurring rate, up to the cap you set |
| Trial start                | Recorded as a trial, with no commission until the first real payment                    |
| Refund                     | Commission reduced in proportion for a partial refund, removed for a full one           |
| Chargeback                 | Commission reversed in full                                                             |
| Discount code              | A sale using an affiliate's code credits them, even without a link                      |
| Test Mode purchase         | Recorded as a test sale, apart from your live commissions, and never paid out           |

Commission is calculated on what the customer paid, or on the price before tax if you switch on
**Commission on the price before tax** in LinkJolt's settings. Every payment is keyed by its Creem
transaction, so webhook retries and manual resends never count twice.

## Refunds and chargebacks

Creem's refund event carries the amount of this refund and what was refunded before it, so LinkJolt
reverses the commission in the same proportion, and a full refund or a chargeback removes it. A
redelivered or out-of-order event never reverses anything twice, and a payout still queued for the
sale is reduced or cancelled to match.

Every commission waits as pending until you approve it, so a refund that arrives first adjusts it
before anything is paid. If you have already paid the commission, LinkJolt still records the
refund, but it does not take money back from the affiliate; settle that with them directly.

## Good to Know

- **The daily check.** Once a day LinkJolt reads its webhook with your key. If Creem switched it
  off after failed deliveries, LinkJolt switches it back on, and if the signing secret changed,
  LinkJolt saves the new one. If the webhook was deleted or the key revoked, the Creem tab says what
  is wrong and LinkJolt emails you once.
- **Short links and storefronts.** The script adds the referral to full payment links and product
  links on your own site. It can't reach a Creem short link (`l.creem.io`) or a storefront page, so
  link to the payment link itself where affiliates send traffic, or give those affiliates a
  discount code.
- **One Creem store per LinkJolt brand.** A store already connected to another brand is refused
  until you disconnect it there.
- **Disconnecting.** Click **Disconnect Creem** on the Creem tab. LinkJolt removes its webhook from
  your store and keeps the discount codes it created, since they live in Creem.
