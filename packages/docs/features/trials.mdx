---
title: "Trials"
description: "Offer free or paid trials to let customers try your subscription products before the regular price applies."
---

A trial is a period at the start of a subscription during which the customer has full access to the product before the regular price is charged. A trial can be **free** or **paid**: a paid trial charges a small fixed amount at checkout, for example $1 for 7 days, then $29 per month.

## Creating a Trial

1. Go to **Products**
2. Create a subscription product, or open an existing one
3. Enable **Trial period** and set the length in days (e.g., 7, 14, or 30 days)
4. Choose **Free** or **Paid**. For a paid trial, set the **trial price** the customer pays at checkout

That's it! New subscriptions to the product start with the trial you configured. Changing the trial later only affects subscriptions created from then on.

## Free Trials

- No payment is collected at checkout; the customer's card is verified and saved for the first payment
- During the trial period, customers have full access to the product
- Customers can cancel anytime during the trial without being charged
- After the trial ends, the subscription automatically begins and the customer is charged the regular price

## Paid Trials

- The trial price is charged at checkout, and the customer's card is saved for the renewals
- During the trial period, customers have full access to the product
- Customers can cancel anytime during the trial; the trial charge is not refunded automatically
- After the trial ends, the subscription renews at the regular price every billing period

The trial price must be at least 1.00 in the product's currency and lower than the regular price. Paid trials are available on subscription products with standard pricing; they cannot be combined with pay-what-you-want pricing, package pricing, or usage-based pricing.

### What the customer sees

The hosted checkout shows the trial charge as **Due today**, followed by the regular price and the date it applies from. The receipt and invoice for the trial charge list the product with its trial length, for example "Pro Plan — 7-day trial".

Tax is calculated on the trial charge like on any other payment, based on the customer's location and the product's tax mode.

### Discount codes

A discount code applied at checkout is applied to the payments after the trial, not to the trial charge. A code that applies once discounts the first payment after the trial; a repeating code discounts that many payments after the trial; a forever code discounts every renewal. The checkout shows the discounted amounts alongside the trial charge.

### Checkout limits

A paid-trial checkout is for a single unit: a checkout created with more than one unit is rejected, and a custom price cannot be combined with a paid trial.

## Subscription Lifecycle

A subscription with a trial has the `trialing` status until the trial ends and moves to `active` with the first regular payment, for free and paid trials alike. Your webhook endpoint receives `subscription.trialing` when the trial starts. See the [subscription lifecycle](/features/subscriptions/introduction#trial-subscription-lifecycle) for the full flow.

## Trials in the API

Products expose their trial configuration, and you can set it when creating or updating a product.

| Field               | Type            | Description                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `trial_period_days` | integer \| null | Length of the trial in whole days, at least 1. `null` when the product has no trial. Recurring products only.      |
| `trial_price`       | integer \| null | Amount charged at checkout to start the trial, in cents. `null` for a free trial and for products without a trial. |

### Create a product with a trial

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.creem.io/v1/products \
  -H "x-api-key: creem_your_api_key" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "Pro Plan",
    "description": "Full access to all features",
    "price": 2900,
    "currency": "USD",
    "billing_type": "recurring",
    "billing_period": "every-month",
    "trial_period_days": 7,
    "trial_price": 100
  }'
```

Omit `trial_price` (or send `0`) for a free trial, and omit `trial_period_days` for no trial. The same validation as the dashboard applies: `trial_price` requires `trial_period_days`, must be at least `100`, and must be lower than `price`.

### Update or remove a trial

```bash
curl -X PATCH https://api.creem.io/v1/products/prod_abc123 \
  -H "x-api-key: creem_your_api_key" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "trial_period_days": 14, "trial_price": 100 }'
```

Every field is optional and omitted fields keep their current value:

- `trial_period_days: 14` sets the trial length (and turns the trial on)
- `trial_price: 100` sets the trial charge; `trial_price: 0` or `null` makes the trial free
- `trial_period_days: null` removes the trial, clearing a paid trial's price with it
- switching `billing_type` to `onetime` removes the trial as well

Products returned by `GET /v1/products/{id}`, `GET /v1/products/search`, checkout sessions and webhook payloads carry the same two fields.
