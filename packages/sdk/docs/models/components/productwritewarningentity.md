                                                                                                                              | What the warning is about. Known values: `usage_prices_shared` (the change of currency or billing period in `path` leaves running subscribers and new buyers sharing the same usage prices, so a later change of a unit price, allowance or cap applies to both), `field_not_writable` (the field in `path` cannot change after create, such as `tax_category`; the value sent was ignored), `reference_not_found` (a dry run only: the key in `key`, sent in `path`, names nothing; the real call answers `400` `reference_not_found`; for a usage price whose `meter_external_id` names no meter, on a create that usage price is left out of the product the dry run returns, and on an update of an existing product the stored usage prices stay as they are). A warning without `impact` means the running subscriptions could not be counted; the write still went through.
