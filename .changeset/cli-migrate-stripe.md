New `creem migrate stripe` copies a Stripe account's Billing Meters and prices into a Creem store. You can run it again: every object is matched by its key.

- It reads the account with a restricted key (`--stripe-key` or `STRIPE_API_KEY`) that can read Products, Prices and Billing Meters, and optionally Webhook Endpoints. It only reads from Stripe, through the official `stripe` SDK, and the key is never sent to Creem, printed or written to the report.
- A Billing Meter with `sum` or `count` becomes a Creem meter keyed by its Stripe id, with the payload key as the aggregation property. Its unit label comes from the Stripe product of a metered price on it, or is the event name, with a warning. A `last` meter is skipped.
- Each price in USD or EUR becomes one Creem product, keyed by its lookup key, or by the price id when the price has no lookup key or Creem doesn't take it as a key; the lookup key is then kept in `metadata.stripe_lookup_key` and the report lists the replacement. The name is the product's, plus the price's nickname or interval when the product has several prices. Preset and custom billing periods, trial days, tax behavior and one-time customer-chosen amounts (as pay what you want) are copied, and the stored metadata is sent back merged with Stripe's product and price metadata, `stripe_product_id` and `stripe_price_id`.
- Metered prices (their meter is still copied), legacy usage-record prices, tiered prices, `transform_quantity` prices, other currencies (with their USD or EUR currency options as a hint) and intervals outside Creem's bounds are skipped, each with its reason. Only active prices and products are read; `--include-archived` writes inactive ones as archived products. Coupons, promotion codes, customers, subscriptions, recorded usage and webhook endpoints are listed as not migrated.
- It works like `creem migrate lemon-squeezy`: Creem checks the whole plan first, every write runs as a dry run right before it, and the report and exit codes are the same. Look-alike Creem products and meters without a key are adopted only when you confirm, in a terminal or with `--adopt`; `--create-new-unmatched` creates new ones instead. Adoption proposals can now be for meters too (`kind` `meter`).
